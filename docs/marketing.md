# Launch plan

Research date: 2026-09-24. This is a proposed launch strategy; channel performance has not been measured.

## Start with TokyoDev

Our first channel to test is the [TokyoDev community](https://www.tokyodev.com/events). Its English-speaking developer audience closely matches this guide. Its official page links to Discord and local meetups. Internal posting rules and permission to share this resource have not been checked.

The promise: **Your first week in Tokyo as a developer or founder—with places to meet people, work, and stay.**

Lead with entry requirements and English access. These help a newcomer take action. GitHub is the home for the guide and community corrections.

## Structure built for newcomers

1. Your first week in Tokyo.
2. Meet developers and founders.
3. Startup hubs and coworking.
4. Cafes and places to work.
5. Where to stay.
6. Find your next event.
7. Contribute and keep it current.

## First four weeks

- Week 1: Publish on GitHub. Read the chosen community's rules and find the appropriate place to share. Ask for corrections to entry requirements.
- Week 2: Apply feedback. Explore whether local organizers find the guide useful for new attendees.
- Week 3: Help people asking relevant questions, with two or three concrete suggestions. Avoid link-only promotion.
- Week 4: Compare useful feedback and available referral data. Choose the next channel based on evidence.

Track GitHub referral sources and unique visitors where available, outside issues/PRs, and voluntary reports of successful visits. Stars are secondary. Record traffic weekly because GitHub retains traffic data for a limited period. Do not treat unattributed visits as known channel conversions.

A proposed first-month target: five useful corrections and three reports that the guide helped someone attend. No results have been measured yet.
