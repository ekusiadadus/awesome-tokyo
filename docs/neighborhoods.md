# Tokyo by neighborhood

[Back to the guide](../README.md). Sources checked 2026-09-24.

Choose an event first, then a work spot and a place to stay. These are suggested combinations, not scheduled tours. Check each booking separately.

## Marunouchi and Yurakucho

**Start here for:** founder support, startup programs, and business meetings around Tokyo Station.

| Place | Exact location | What to do | Entry and language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Startup Hub Tokyo Marunouchi](https://startup-station.jp/m1/) | Meiji Yasuda Life Building, low-rise wing 1F, 2-1-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda. [Access](https://startup-station.jp/access/) | Work on an early business idea, attend a founder event, or book a consultation. | Register as a member. Free support services; mainly Japanese information. Confirm English support before booking. |
| [Tokyo Innovation Base (TIB)](https://tib.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en) | 3-8-3 Marunouchi, Chiyoda; near Yurakucho Station. | Attend startup events and explore support programs. | Membership registration for services; individual event rules apply. English information; session language varies. |
| [EGG](https://www.egg-japan.com/en/) | Shin-Marunouchi Building, 10F reception, 1-5-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda; Tokyo Station's Marunouchi North Exit. | Explore an ongoing workspace and founder/business network. | The official English site describes an invitation-only community. Contact the operator; do not expect public drop-in access. |

**Work:** Startup Hub Tokyo's [lounge](https://startup-station.jp/m1/service/) is for startup work and meetings, with Wi-Fi and power. The operator asks visitors to keep use to roughly three hours. It is a founder-support facility; check that your intended use fits.

**Stay:** [THE GATE HOTEL TOKYO](https://www.gate-hotel.jp/en/tokyo/) in Yurakucho. For shared work and kitchen facilities, compare [lyf Ginza Tokyo](https://www.discoverasr.com/en/lyf/japan/lyf-ginza-tokyo) in nearby Kyobashi, across in Chuo ward.

**A useful sequence:** a booked founder consultation or work session at Startup Hub Tokyo, then a separately registered TIB event. EGG requires an arranged visit.

## Shibuya

**Start here for:** international founder events, creative projects, and a choice of workspaces.

### Around Shibuya Station

- [SHIBUYA QWS](https://shibuya-qws.com/en): project community and events at Shibuya Scramble Square **15F**. Workspace access follows its [membership plans](https://shibuya-qws.com/en/membership); check public event tickets separately. English information is available, but event language varies. Use the [office entrance](https://shibuya-qws.com/en/about/outline), not the retail elevators.
- [SHARE LOUNGE](https://www.sharelounge.jp/store/2330): paid work lounge in the same building on **11F**. A practical option for Wi-Fi, power, and a short work session. This is separate from QWS.
- Google for Startups Campus Tokyo: see the [access caveat](../README.md#startup-hubs-and-coworking). Do not build a day around an unconfirmed visit.

### Dogenzaka, Jinnan, and Tomigaya

- [FabCafe Tokyo](https://fabcafe.com/jp/tokyo/), Dogenzaka: coffee or a creative event. Confirm the event's admission and language.
- [.andwork Shibuya](https://www.livelyhotels.com/en/themillennialsshibuya/faq/), at The Millennials in Jinnan: paid work sessions for non-guests, subject to registration and availability.
- [Fuglen Tokyo](https://fuglencoffee.jp/pages/fuglen-tokyo), 1-16-11 Tomigaya: a coffee conversation or an evening drink. This is a different pocket of Shibuya; check the route before arranging back-to-back meetings. Laptop facilities are unverified.

### Shibuya Bridge / Higashi

[Shibuya Startup Support](https://www.shibuya-startup-support.jp/events) lists its space at **Shibuya Bridge B-5, 1-29-3 Higashi**. Follow the calendar for international founder events; individual events may use other venues. Confirm registration before visiting.

**Stay:** [HOTEL GRAPHY SHIBUYA](https://www.livelyhotels.com/en/graphyshibuya/rooms/). Compare dorms, shared-bath private rooms, and ensuite rooms carefully.

**A useful sequence:** a paid work session near the station, coffee at FabCafe, then a confirmed QWS or Shibuya Startup Support event. Choose based on that week's calendar.

## Toranomon and Shimbashi

**Start here for:** meeting founders, investors, and researchers through startup events.

- [Venture Café Tokyo](https://venturecafetokyo.org/): Thursday Gathering at **CIC Tokyo, Toranomon Hills Business Tower 15F, 1-17-1 Toranomon**. Free with advance registration, subject to capacity. Check the date and session language. Follow the [visitor instructions](https://venturecafetokyo.org/about/faq/) from the building's 1F office lobby.
- [CIC Tokyo](https://jp.cic.com/en/cic-tokyo/): paid coworking and offices at the same address, with bilingual staff. Workspace membership is separate from an event ticket.
- [BasisPoint Shimbashi Ginza-guchi](https://basispoint.tokyo/store/coworking/shimbashi/): paid drop-in workspace near Shimbashi Station. Wi-Fi and power are listed; conversation and calls are allowed.

**Stay:** [Hotel Toranomon Hills](https://www.hyatt.com/unbound-collection/en-US/tyoub-hotel-toranomon-hills). The hotel and CIC are separate destinations; use the event's building instructions.

**A useful sequence:** work in Shimbashi, then attend a registered Venture Café session. Some Thursdays have no gathering.

## Follow the community, not its name

Developer groups often move between company offices or meet online. A name such as Shibuya.rb or Asakusa.rb does not guarantee today's venue is in that neighborhood. See [Japanese-speaking developer communities](../README.md#if-you-speak-japanese) and use the current event's address.
